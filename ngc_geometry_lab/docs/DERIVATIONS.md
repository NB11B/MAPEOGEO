## 5. Level 3: Derivation of the Born Probability Law

Rather than positing \(p_i = |\langle \psi_i | \psi \rangle|^2\) as an independent axiom, we derive it from geometry and operational consistency.

### Theorem 3.1 (Lean Geometric Born Rule for Binary Measurements)
Assume only three operational conditions:
1. **State Geometry:** Physical states are vectors $r$ within the unit ball $\mathcal{B}_1 = \{r \in \mathbb{R}^3 : \|r\| \le 1\}$; pure states satisfy $\|r\| = 1$.
2. **Mixture Consistency (Affinity):** Outcome probabilities of a statistical mixture equal the mixture of outcome probabilities:
   \[
   p(+\mid p r_1 + (1-p) r_2, n) = p\,p(+\mid r_1, n) + (1-p)\,p(+\mid r_2, n).
   \]
   Hence $p(+\mid r, n)$ must be an affine function of $r$:
   \[
   p(+\mid r, n) = \alpha + \beta \cdot r.
   \]
3. **Sharp Endpoint Calibration:**
   - Aligned pure state along unit detector direction $n$ yields positive outcome with certainty:
     \[
     p(+\mid n, n) = \alpha + \beta \cdot n = 1.
     \]
   - Anti-aligned pure state yields negative outcome with certainty:
     \[
     p(+\mid -n, n) = \alpha - \beta \cdot n = 0.
     \]

Adding and subtracting these two calibration equations yields:
\[
2\alpha = 1 \implies \alpha = \frac{1}{2}, \qquad 2(\beta \cdot n) = 1 \implies \beta \cdot n = \frac{1}{2}.
\]

**Global Validity Bound:**
For $p(+\mid r, n)$ to be a valid probability ($0 \le p \le 1$) for **every** state in the unit ball $\|r\| \le 1$, its minimum $\alpha - \|\beta\|$ must be non-negative:
\[
\alpha - \|\beta\| \ge 0 \implies \|\beta\| \le \alpha = \frac{1}{2}.
\]
By Cauchy-Schwarz:
\[
\frac{1}{2} = \beta \cdot n \le \|\beta\| \|n\| = \|\beta\| \cdot 1 = \|\beta\| \le \frac{1}{2}.
\]
Because the upper bound is saturated ($\beta \cdot n = \|\beta\| \|n\|$), the vector $\beta$ must be strictly parallel to $n$ with magnitude $\|\beta\| = \frac{1}{2}$:
\[
\beta = \frac{1}{2} n.
\]
Substituting back into the affine function yields:
\[
\boxed{p(+\mid r, n) = \frac{1 + r \cdot n}{2}}.
\]

> **Deductive Economy:** Rotational covariance is **not** an independent premise. Affinity, sharp calibration, and validity throughout the unit ball uniquely determine the Born rule.

### Corollary 3.2 (Quadratic Cosine Law for Pure States)
For pure states $\|r\| = 1$ with $r \cdot n = \cos\theta$:
\[
p(+\mid r, n) = \frac{1 + \cos\theta}{2} = \cos^2\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right).
\]
For general mixed states $\|r\| < 1$, the linear dot-product form is retained directly without ad-hoc modification.

### Theorem 4.2 (General Tsirelson Bound for Arbitrary Measurement Angles)
Consider the bilinear correlation model $E(a, b) = a^T T b$ for arbitrary unit detector directions $\|a\| = \|a'\| = \|b\| = \|b'\| = 1$. 

Valid physical correlations require $|E(a, b)| \le 1$ for all unit directions, which implies the operator norm bound:
\[
\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le 1.
\]

The CHSH quantity across four arbitrary unit vectors is:
\[
S = a^T T (b + b') + (a')^T T (b - b').
\]
Applying Cauchy-Schwarz and operator norm bounds:
\[
|S| \le \|a\| \|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \|b + b'\| + \|a'\| \|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \|b - b'\| \le \|b + b'\| + \|b - b'\|.
\]

By the parallelogram identity on Euclidean space:
\[
\|b + b'\|^2 + \|b - b'\|^2 = 2(\|b\|^2 + \|b'\|^2) = 2(1 + 1) = 4.
\]

Now apply the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality to the 2D vector $(\|b + b'\|, \|b - b'\|)$ with $(1, 1)$:
\[
(\|b + b'\| + \|b - b'\|)^2 \le (1^2 + 1^2)\left(\|b + b'\|^2 + \|b - b'\|^2\right) = 2 \times 4 = 8.
\]
Taking the square root:
\[
\boxed{|S| \le \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.828427}.
\]

> **Geometric Meaning:** This bound holds for **arbitrary** measurement settings $a, a', b, b'$, without assuming orthogonality a priori.
