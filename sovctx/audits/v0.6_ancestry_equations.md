## Pre-Soviet ancestry layer

Added namespace `ANC` and ancestry-specific provenance classes `A1-A5`.

Documented paths now qualify:

```
Markov -> Chebyshev -> Euler -> Leibniz
Lyapunov -> Chebyshev -> Euler -> Leibniz
```

Evidence:
- Euler's own geometry-of-position paper explicitly attributes `Geometria situs` to Leibniz.
- V. V. Kozlov's Russian Mathematical Surveys institutional history states that Chebyshev actively edited and published Euler's works and identifies Markov and Lyapunov among Chebyshev's students.

These are transmission/ancestry edges only. They do not assert that every later theorem descends mathematically from Leibniz.
