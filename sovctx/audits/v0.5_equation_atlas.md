## Equation contract

Each equation may carry:
- original and normalized notation
- source and exact source fragments
- symbols and source-native meanings
- assumptions
- operator classifications
- invariants
- behavioral tests
- dimensional annotations
- transcription/verification status

Behavioral qualification is explicitly independent of truth/proof qualification.
